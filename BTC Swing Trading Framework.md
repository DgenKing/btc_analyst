# BTC Swing Trading Framework

## Overview

This framework outlines a structured Bitcoin swing trading strategy focused on:

- Support and resistance
- Volume profile analysis
- Range trading
- Multi-timeframe analysis
- Trendline confluence
- High-probability leveraged trades
- Patience and selective execution

The objective is not constant trading.

The objective is:
- To wait patiently for high-probability setups
- To trade only when multiple factors align
- To capture medium-sized directional moves during the trading week
- To preserve capital and avoid unnecessary exposure

---

# Core Philosophy

The strategy is based around:

1. Patience
2. Market structure
3. Support and resistance
4. Volume profile confluence
5. Trend continuation and rejection
6. Risk management
7. Low trade frequency
8. Multi-timeframe alignment
9. Trading reactions, not predictions
10. Preserving capital first

# Weekly Trading Cycle

## Friday / Weekend Strategy

The market often becomes:
- Lower liquidity
- More erratic
- Less reliable
- More prone to fake moves

The framework avoids unnecessary weekend exposure.

### Thursday / Friday
- Reduce exposure where appropriate
- Close trades if market conditions weaken
- Avoid forcing new setups
- Prepare levels for the next week

### Saturday
- Observation only
- No emotional trades
- Build possible scenarios
- Mark key levels

### Sunday (10 PM GMT)
- Futures/opening liquidity returns
- Begin analysing market structure
- Look for potential setups
- Decide whether conditions justify a trade

# Support & Resistance

Support and resistance are the foundation of the framework.

These are the most important levels in the market.

## Horizontal Levels

Horizontal levels carry the highest importance because they represent:
- Historical reactions
- Market memory
- Liquidity concentration
- Institutional interest
- Repeated defended areas

These levels are considered stronger than diagonal trendlines.

---

# Volume Profile Analysis

Volume profile is one of the core components of the strategy.

The focus is heavily placed on:
- High Volume Nodes (HVNs)
- Point of Control (POC)
- Value Areas
- Acceptance and rejection zones

---

## High Volume Nodes (HVNs)

HVNs represent areas where:
- Large trading volume occurred
- The market spent significant time
- Buyers and sellers found agreement

These areas often become:
- Strong support
- Strong resistance
- Reaccumulation zones
- Distribution zones

---

## Point of Control (POC)

The Point of Control is one of the most important areas within the framework.

### Definition
The POC is:
- The price level with the highest traded volume within the selected range

### Why It Matters
The POC represents:
- Market acceptance
- Fair value
- Institutional participation
- High liquidity

Price often reacts strongly around the POC.

---

# Trendlines

The framework uses both:
- Horizontal trendlines
- Diagonal trendlines

However, they do not carry equal weight.

---

## Horizontal Trendlines
Highest importance.

Reason:
- More objective
- Based on historical price agreement
- Stronger market memory
- More reliable support/resistance

---

## Diagonal Trendlines
Secondary importance.

Used for:
- Trend direction
- Channels
- Momentum analysis
- Compression patterns
- Breakout structures

Diagonal trendlines are considered weaker than horizontal levels because:
- They are more subjective
- Easier to break
- Depend on interpretation and angle

A diagonal trendline alone is not enough for a trade.

# Range Trading Structure

The framework heavily relies on range analysis.

Markets often move:
- From one range into another
- From acceptance into expansion
- From support into resistance

---

## Example

### Range 1
102k - 106k

If BTC breaks above 106k and holds:
- Range 2 becomes active

### Range 2
106k - 112k

Key principle:
- Old resistance becomes support
- Acceptance above range = bullish
- Failure back into old range = weakness

---

# Acceptance vs Rejection

The strategy focuses heavily on:
- Market acceptance
- Market rejection

This is more important than individual candles.

---

## Bullish Acceptance
Signs:
- Holding support
- Strong reclaim
- Volume building above range
- Acceptance above key levels

Result:
Potential continuation higher

---

## Bearish Rejection
Signs:
- Rejection at resistance
- Failure reclaim
- Breakdown below support
- Weak momentum

Result:
Potential continuation lower

# Long Setup Framework

A long setup typically requires:

- Support holding
- Range support
- HVN or POC support
- Trendline support
- Higher timeframe bullish bias

- Momentum confirmation

# Short Setup Framework

A short setup typically requires:

- Resistance rejection
- Breakdown below support
- Failure reclaim
- Weak momentum
- Bearish higher timeframe structure
- Rejection from HVN or POC
- Trendline rejection
